# Clavis demo storyboard

Target duration: 60–90 seconds, with the exact cut derived from ElevenLabs character timestamps.

| Beat | Visual | Narration focus | Product proof |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Promise | Landing hero and live policy card | Speed without losing control | Private, rule-bound payroll positioning |
| 2. Configure | Owner setup wizard: limits, recipients, agent | Define policy once | Treasury limits, personal caps, schedules, routes |
| 3. Constrain | Owner dashboard and rules engine | Restricted autonomous agent | Function Call Key limited to `request_payment` |
| 4. Enforce | Treasury, roster, anomaly alert, policy controls | Independent onchain checks | Active recipient, schedule, caps, daily limit, registered route |
| 5. Fail closed | Audit decision history | Approval creates an intent; violations are rejected | Public, permanent contract decisions |
| 6. Prove privately | Audit privacy statement, then worker portal | Proof without salary exposure | Wallet-scoped worker view and private delivery data |
| 7. Close | Landing architecture section and branded end card | Enough authority to work, none to break rules | NEAR-native agent, contract, executor, and Intents loop |

Production direction:

- 1920×1080, 30 fps, H.264/AAC MP4.
- OBS preserves the uninterrupted source walkthrough.
- Remotion adds scene wipes, restrained camera motion, product callouts, word-synchronized captions, title treatment, and end card.
- Captions and scene timing are generated from the same ElevenLabs alignment response as the narration audio.
- The browser starts from a fresh profile and seeds only public testnet account IDs. No wallet private key is loaded into the browser.
