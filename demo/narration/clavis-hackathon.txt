Meet Clavis: privacy-minimized, rule-bound payroll on NEAR testnet.

Employers set approved workers, schedules, caps, treasury limits, and one executable native NEAR route.

A restricted agent runs each cycle. Its Function Call key can request payment on one contract—and nothing else.

The contract checks identity, timing, caps, daily spend, route, and the recipient commitment onchain.

In source v0.2, approved native payments move directly from the contract and finalize only after transfer success. Unsupported cross-chain requests fail closed.

The public audit proves employer-wide decisions without repeating salaries, recipient identities, or routes. Normal NEAR transaction data remains public.

Clavis gives automation enough authority to work, and none to break the rules.
