Meet Clavis: private, rule-bound payroll on NEAR.

Employers set approved workers, schedules, caps, treasury limits, and delivery routes once.

A restricted AI agent runs each cycle. Its Function Call Key can request payment on one contract—and nothing else.

The contract checks identity, timing, caps, daily spend, and route onchain.

Approved requests become cross-chain intents. Any violation fails closed, even if the agent is compromised.

The public audit proves every decision without exposing salaries, names, wallets, or chains. Workers see only their own schedule and route.

Clavis gives automation enough authority to work, and none to break the rules.
